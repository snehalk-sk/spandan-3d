THE IDEA

Turning digital ideas into real objects.

3D printing makes it possible to take a digital design and create it physically, one layer at a time. Spandan 3D is built around using that process for products that can be displayed, gifted, customized or created for a specific requirement.

Instead of filling the website with categories before products are actually available, the collection can grow naturally as new models are printed and prepared.

Customers who already have a model or an idea can also send their requirements through the custom printing page. The request can then be reviewed before a price is confirmed.

Ready products or your own idea. Shop the products already available, or send a custom request when you need something different.
